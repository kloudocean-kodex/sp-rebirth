# SP_REBIRTH · Sana handwritten discovery

Status: source-derived project input captured from Sana's handwritten notes and screenshots shared during discovery. This file records intent and hypotheses; it is not automatically publishable copy.

## Core owner diagnostic themes

Sana repeatedly frames property management as an active operating discipline rather than rent collection. The recurring questions in her notes are:

1. When was the property's rent last strategically reviewed?
2. Are routine inspections genuinely protecting the property or merely ticking a box?
3. Are maintenance issues being resolved before they become more expensive problems?
4. How quickly does the current agent provide a useful response?
5. Does the rental provider know when the current agreement expires and what the next decision is?
6. Does the owner know whether required safety/compliance records are current, including relevant gas, electrical and smoke-alarm checks?
7. Is the property being managed proactively or reactively?
8. Is the property sitting vacant longer than it reasonably should?

These are excellent conversion questions because they expose uncertainty without requiring a visitor to declare that their current agent is bad.

## Sana's strategic-management model

Her notes map service activity to an owner outcome:

- Rent optimisation -> market-based rental reviews and longer-term rental positioning.
- Renter selection -> quality selection informed by rental/employment history and suitability.
- Inspections -> property protection, not box-ticking.
- Maintenance -> early intervention and sensible expenditure.
- Compliance -> proactively tracked obligations and records.
- Communication -> direct access and clear advice.
- Rent disbursements -> timely handling and visibility.

Public copy must avoid guarantees, absolute legal-compliance claims, or unsupported statements about what every renter/owner outcome will be.

### Confirmed operating promise — 27 August 2026

Sana has confirmed that she is willing to fulfil the handwritten **24×7 direct access to Sana Patel** promise. This is now a confirmed SP_REBIRTH service requirement rather than an unverified claim.

Public wording should preserve an important operational distinction: **24×7 direct access means the rental provider has a direct path to Sana when something needs attention; it does not mean every repair, trade attendance, legal/process step or final resolution can be completed instantly at any hour.**

The future operating model should investigate automation and AI-assisted triage, acknowledgement, prioritisation, context capture and escalation so that the promise remains responsive at scale without pretending automation is Sana. Human accountability stays with Sana; automation should support it.

## Guardrails

- Do not copy competitor wording.
- Do not publish review, performance, vacancy, rental-growth or case-study numbers without source evidence.
- Do not describe an automated tool as a valuation, compliance audit or legal review.
- Do not imply rent should always be increased at a particular interval.
- Compliance content must link to current Victorian primary/authoritative sources and carry a review date.
- Diagnostic funnels should create clarity, not fear.
- Do not imply that AI or automation is Sana, a licensed professional or a human response when it is not.
- For urgent renter safety or emergency situations, never rely solely on an AI/chat/web workflow; official emergency and urgent-repair pathways must remain clear.
