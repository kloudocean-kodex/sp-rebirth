# SP_REBIRTH Reference Taste Study

These references are used to understand Sana's taste, not as templates to copy.

## Sixth Vision

What to borrow:

- cinematic, property-first storytelling
- strong hero statement paired with immediate action
- dramatic but restrained dark/light contrast
- warm dusk/golden-hour imagery
- large editorial headings with generous breathing room
- proof placed close to commercial claims
- clear process and outcome sections
- rich visuals balanced with plain-language CTAs

What not to copy:

- aggressive guarantees or promotional urgency unsuitable for property management
- image-dense portfolio rhythm where Sana needs trust/decision clarity
- the exact visual identity or typography

## Sawyer Sanctuary

What to borrow:

- calm, human pace
- founder-led warmth
- sense of safety and personal connection
- softer language and emotional permission to start a conversation
- breathing space around personal story

What not to copy:

- wellness/spiritual visual tropes
- overly delicate or low-contrast typography
- language that would make Sana feel less commercially authoritative

## NorthEdge redesign repository

The current public NorthEdge domain does not represent the warm redesign Sana liked. The reviewed reference is the
`kloudocean-kodex/Northedge-website` repository rendered locally on 1 September 2026.

What to borrow:

- warm ivory foundations with eucalyptus and restrained brass rather than a black-dominant luxury palette
- a real place-led hero, generous editorial spacing and a calm serif/sans hierarchy
- selective use of a deep green or ink surface to ground the page instead of making every section dark
- locally meaningful architecture and aerial imagery with a clear role in the story
- an understated footer and call-to-action rhythm

What not to copy:

- the exact Jost / Cormorant pairing; SP_REBIRTH retains Newsreader / DM Sans
- its exact colours, layout, content model or brand devices
- any property, founder or performance implication that Sana's evidence does not support

## Rhyco Homes

What to borrow:

- craftsmanship / material quality feeling
- project-led credibility rather than decorative luxury
- local Melbourne confidence
- founder/director accountability throughout the process
- transparent, personable service language
- work quality and customer testimony as the centre of proof

What not to copy:

- builder/project-gallery information architecture
- construction-specific language or literal material palette

## RISE with Sarah Cincotta — Sana-supplied priority reference, 3 September 2026

Sana specifically pointed to RISE with Sarah Cincotta after reviewing SP_REBIRTH on both mobile and laptop. Her comments
identified two parts of the RISE treatment she wanted translated into her own site: the cut-out/organic treatment of
founder photography and the strong black/white/accent colour system. Sana proposed black, white and golden rather than
RISE's black, white and orange.

The current public RISE homepage, mentor page, programme page, resources page and contact page were reviewed alongside
Sana's supplied WhatsApp captures. Public sources checked:

- https://risewithsarahcincotta.com.au/
- https://risewithsarahcincotta.com.au/meet-your-mentor/
- https://risewithsarahcincotta.com.au/the-rise-journey/rise-8-week-bdm-program/
- https://risewithsarahcincotta.com.au/resources/
- https://risewithsarahcincotta.com.au/connect/

### What Sana appears to be responding to visually

- a confident near-black navigation/header band rather than a soft, blended luxury header
- strong black/white contrast with one warm accent colour used for emphasis
- a founder/mentor shown as a recognisable visual anchor instead of relying on property imagery alone
- portraits placed inside circles, arches and irregular/organic masks, which breaks the rectangular-template feeling
- short, bold heading moments that can be understood before reading supporting paragraphs
- clear separation between visual sections, making a long page feel more like a sequence of distinct moments
- graphic brand devices and oversized simple forms that make the page memorable without requiring animation
- prominent calls to action placed alongside the founder narrative rather than hidden behind generic navigation
- proof, video and recognition used as supporting trust layers after the visitor understands the person and offer

### Important distinction: visual inspiration, not information architecture

The public RISE site itself has a deep navigation tree and substantial long-form copy. Sana's feedback on SP_REBIRTH was
the opposite on information density: she said the current site felt repetitive, reading-heavy and disorienting because
clicks kept taking her somewhere else. Therefore the RISE reference must not be interpreted as permission to reproduce
its navigation depth or page volume.

The correct translation is:

- borrow RISE's visual confidence, human focal point, cut-out composition and black/white/accent discipline
- make SP_REBIRTH materially simpler than RISE in primary navigation and required reading
- keep high-intent/SEO/supporting routes underneath the simplified experience rather than placing them all in the header
- use direct phone contact as a first-class action because Sana explicitly described that as the behaviour she would take
  when urgently looking for a property manager

### SP_REBIRTH translation of the RISE grammar

- near-black header and intentional dark anchor sections
- white/ivory reading fields to keep long-form readability calm
- champagne/brass/gold as the single warm accent family; no bright yellow-gold and no metallic text for body copy
- a large founder portrait composition using an arch/organic mask and a simple gold halo/rule treatment
- bolder DM Sans headline moments in the hero and contact sections, with Newsreader retained selectively for editorial
  warmth so the result is not a RISE clone
- primary homepage navigation reduced to Services, Why Sana, Reviews, direct phone access and Rental Appraisal
- supporting routes retained in the footer and as contextual links for SEO/discoverability
- diagnostic GrowthEngine tools retained as useful campaign/resource assets but removed from the homepage's primary path
- no autoplay hero video; the premium static-media reliability contract remains

### Photography rule after Sana's feedback

Sana explicitly rejected the current website photographs and wants them changed. Until a new approved photography session
is available:

- concept/staging may use clearly non-evidentiary generated or illustrated placeholders to prove composition
- placeholders must not be presented as photographs of Sana or evidence of a managed property
- production requires genuine approved Sana photography for founder/profile imagery
- the future shoot must deliberately capture cut-out-friendly full and three-quarter portraits, landscape working frames,
  mobile crops and negative-space compositions so the RISE-inspired visual grammar can be executed authentically

## Combined Sana taste profile

The overlap is now **cinematic + human + crafted + graphically confident**.

SP_REBIRTH should therefore feel:

- visually rich without being visually busy
- premium and decisive without generic luxury clichés
- founder-led without becoming influencer branding
- image-led where emotion and recognition matter
- text-led only where decisions and trust require explanation
- crafted rather than templated
- immediately understandable to an owner who wants to call rather than browse

## Translation into SP_REBIRTH

### Typography

- DM Sans can take a stronger role in large homepage statements where directness is more important than editorial mood
- Newsreader remains for selected editorial headings, pull quotes and human storytelling
- restrained weights, strong hierarchy and short line lengths
- headings must communicate the section meaning even when body copy is skipped

### Colour

- near-black as the primary header and selected high-impact anchor surface
- white/ivory as the dominant reading field
- brushed brass / champagne / deep bronze as the only warm accent family
- the overall impression may read as black / white / gold while still keeping accessible warm reading surfaces
- no bright yellow-gold, body text in gold, or full-page black-and-gold luxury-template treatment

### Photography

- Sana must become the primary human visual differentiator once approved new photography exists
- cut-out, arch and organic crops may create the visual energy Sana liked in RISE
- property/place imagery remains secondary context, not a substitute for founder recognition
- generated imagery is limited to non-evidentiary concept review; it must not fabricate Sana, clients, managed properties,
  listings, inspections or results

### Layout

- one strong opening message and one or two immediate actions
- a short set of owner situations instead of competing exploratory journeys
- founder story and service proof combined where possible rather than repeated across adjacent sections
- generous negative space with strong graphic separation between sections
- proof and next action close to every major claim

### Emotional sequence

1. Presence — this looks intentional and premium
2. Recognition — this is clearly Melbourne property management and clearly Sana
3. Clarity — I understand what she does without reading a long explanation
4. Trust — I can see who is accountable and inspect current proof
5. Action — I can call Sana or request an appraisal immediately

## Non-negotiables

- preserve Sana's original logo/wordmark
- do not imitate any reference site's exact styling, assets, copy, typography or proprietary brand devices
- do not invent review counts, awards, statistics, properties or client outcomes
- do not use a synthetic Sana likeness as production founder evidence
- accessibility and conversion clarity outrank decorative styling
- mobile must retain the same sense of quality as desktop
- production promotion remains a separate explicit gate
