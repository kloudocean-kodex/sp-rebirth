# SP_REBIRTH Reference Taste Study

These references are used to understand Sana's taste, not as templates to copy.

## Sixth Vision

What to borrow:

- cinematic, property-first storytelling
- strong hero statement paired with immediate action
- dramatic but restrained dark/light contrast
- warm dusk/golden-hour imagery
- large editorial headings with generous breathing room
- proof placed close to commercial claims
- clear process and outcome sections
- rich visuals balanced with plain-language CTAs

What not to copy:

- aggressive guarantees or promotional urgency unsuitable for property management
- image-dense portfolio rhythm where Sana needs trust/decision clarity
- the exact visual identity or typography

## Sawyer Sanctuary

What to borrow:

- calm, human pace
- founder-led warmth
- sense of safety and personal connection
- softer language and emotional permission to start a conversation
- breathing space around personal story

What not to copy:

- wellness/spiritual visual tropes
- overly delicate or low-contrast typography
- language that would make Sana feel less commercially authoritative

## Rhyco Homes

What to borrow:

- craftsmanship / material quality feeling
- project-led credibility rather than decorative luxury
- local Melbourne confidence
- founder/director accountability throughout the process
- transparent, personable service language
- work quality and customer testimony as the centre of proof

What not to copy:

- builder/project-gallery information architecture
- construction-specific language or literal material palette

## Combined Sana taste profile

The overlap is **cinematic + human + crafted**.

SP_REBIRTH should therefore feel:

- visually rich without being visually busy
- warm without becoming soft or beige
- premium without luxury clichés
- founder-led without becoming influencer branding
- image-led where emotion matters
- text-led where decisions and trust matter
- crafted rather than templated

## Translation into SP_REBIRTH

### Typography

- Newsreader for editorial warmth and authority
- DM Sans for clean, modern, high-legibility navigation, body and forms
- restrained weights, optical sizing and short line lengths

### Colour

- limestone / warm ivory base
- charcoal / near-black anchor surfaces
- parchment section contrast
- brushed brass / champagne details
- deep bronze only for accessible interactive states
- no bright yellow-gold and no black-and-gold cliché

### Photography

- Sana and real property/lifestyle imagery should carry more weight than generic icons
- dusk / golden-hour / warm interior imagery can support Melbourne real-estate context
- image crops should feel editorial, not catalogue-like

### Layout

- large cinematic opening moments
- asymmetric founder/story sections
- restrained grids for services/process
- generous negative space
- proof and next action close to every major claim

### Emotional sequence

1. Presence — this feels considered and premium
2. Recognition — this understands my frustrations as an owner
3. Trust — Sana appears personally accountable and competent
4. Clarity — I understand what happens next
5. Action — appraisal, switching conversation or direct contact

## Non-negotiables

- preserve Sana's original logo/wordmark
- do not imitate any reference site's exact styling
- do not invent review counts, awards, statistics or client outcomes
- accessibility and conversion clarity outrank decorative styling
- mobile must retain the same sense of quality as desktop
