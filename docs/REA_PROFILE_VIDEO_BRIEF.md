# Sana Patel REA profile video brief

Status: production-ready recording/edit brief; no video is published and no synthetic Sana likeness is approved.

## Purpose

Create a genuine 45–60 second founder introduction for Sana's realestate.com.au agent profile, YouTube, the future website
media library and short social cut-downs. The video should establish Sana, her operating philosophy and a clear next step.
It is not a generic property montage, testimonial substitute or performance advertisement.

Working title:

**Meet Sana Patel | Clear, accountable property management across Melbourne**

## Spoken script

> Property management should never leave an owner guessing.
>
> I'm Sana Patel, Managing Director and Officer in Effective Control of Sana Patel Real Estate. I work directly with
> rental providers across Melbourne to make the management of their investment visible, considered and accountable.
>
> For me, good management means knowing who is responsible, what has changed, why it matters and what happens next. That
> applies whether we are preparing a property for lease, reviewing its rental position, coordinating maintenance or
> planning an orderly change of manager.
>
> Every property, tenancy and owner objective is different, so the first step is understanding your situation and
> explaining the options clearly.
>
> If you would value a confidential conversation or a no-obligation rental appraisal, I would be pleased to help.

Target delivery: calm, conversational and direct; approximately 125–135 spoken words. Do not rush to force an exact
duration.

## Recording specification

- landscape 16:9; record 4K if available, 25 or 30 fps, with the lens at eye level
- frame Sana near the centre-safe region so REA, YouTube and 9:16 social crops remain possible
- use a quiet, softly lit property/office location; avoid a bright window directly behind Sana
- use a lapel or close directional microphone and record ten seconds of room tone
- capture three complete script takes plus natural pauses; do not stitch every sentence into a visibly artificial cadence
- remove documents, keys, screens, addresses and personal information that could identify a renter, owner or property
- obtain permission for every identifiable person and non-public property shown

## Edit and accessibility contract

- master: 3840×2160 or 1920×1080 H.264 MP4, 16:9, stereo AAC; web derivative only after quality review
- use restrained cuts and minimal motion; no artificial camera shake, luxury clichés or loud motivational music
- accurate human-reviewed English captions plus a plain transcript are required
- keep music, if any, materially below speech and verify dialogue on phone speakers and headphones
- use the authentic Sana Patel logo; do not redraw, recolour or regenerate it
- thumbnail: 1280×720, real Sana, uncluttered dark/ivory background, six words or fewer, no rating/award/performance claim
- publish to YouTube as unlisted for approval first; after Sana approves picture, captions, description and claims, make it
  public and add the exact YouTube URL to REA
- retain a static poster and complete text alternative; video must never be required to understand the service or convert
